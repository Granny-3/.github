## Welcome to Your Worst Nightmare  

**Granny and Grandpa** have moved into a new, sinister mansion—surrounded by a moat and towering walls. But don’t be fooled by its aged elegance; this place is a prison, and **you’re the prey**.  

### The Story Unfolds  
You’re an explorer, drawn to an abandoned mansion under the cloak of night. The gate creaks open, unlocked—a silent invitation. But inside lurks **Granny**, the infamous horror, and her equally terrifying husband, **Grandpa**. Their goal? To keep you trapped **forever**.  

Your mission is simple: **Escape**.

## Gameplay Mechanics  

### Stealth & Survival  
- **Granny hears everything**—dropped items, creaking floors, careless footsteps. Make noise, and she’ll come **running**.  
- **Grandpa is half-deaf but sharp-eyed**. If he spots you, **run**.  
- Hide under **beds, couches, or wardrobes**—but stay silent. If they catch you hiding, it’s game over.  

### Tools & Traps  
- Scavenge the mansion for **useful items**—keys, weapons, and tools to aid your escape.  
- **Bear traps** lurk in the shadows. Step into one, and you’ll regret it.  
- Fight back with **weapons**, but they only stun Granny and Grandpa **temporarily**.  

### Difficulty Levels  
| Difficulty   | Description                                                                 |  
|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|  
| **Extreme**  | Granny is faster, smarter, and deadlier. Only for the bravest.             |  
| **Hard**     | A tough challenge with limited resources.                                  |  
| **Normal**   | Balanced gameplay—tense but fair.                                          |  
| **Easy**     | Granny is slower; good for beginners.                                      |  
| **Practice** | No Granny or Grandpa… but the house still holds dangers. Learn the ropes!  |  

### The Clock is Ticking  
You have **five days** to escape. Fail, and… well, let’s just say **Granny has plans for you**.
